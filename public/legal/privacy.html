PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective June 23, 2026

Data we collect

LineUp collects account email, display name, optional profile photo, campus selection, favorite venues, launch-deal requests, structured crowd reports, reward activity, notification preference, and owner or staff role information when applicable.

Optional location

If you allow location while using LineUp, we collect foreground coordinates, device-reported accuracy, nearby venue distance, and presence or check-in signals. Location is optional. You can select University of Arizona and browse manually without granting location.

Device, analytics, and diagnostics

We use signed-device and session identifiers, venue/detail/deal interactions, diagnostics, request metadata, IP information processed during network requests, and limited security logs to operate the service, prevent abuse, measure aggregate interest, and improve crowd estimates.

How we use and share data

Data supports accounts, favorites, reports, rewards, deal requests, safety, fraud prevention, aggregate analytics, and product reliability. Venue operators may receive aggregate deal-interest and performance totals. They do not receive raw launch-request identities or individual location histories. Supabase, Mapbox, and hosting/network providers process data needed to operate LineUp.

Retention and deletion

Operational records are retained only as needed for service operation, safety, fraud prevention, and aggregate analytics. In Profile > Account & Access, you can delete your account. Deletion removes your profile, favorites, saved devices, precise presence and check-in history, rewards, launch-deal requests, and reports. Non-identifying aggregate venue statistics may remain.

Your choices

You may use anonymous display mode, decline location and notifications, select campus manually, change preferences, or delete your account in the app.

Contact

Email privacy and account questions to support@get-lineup.app. Do not send passwords or precise location data.
