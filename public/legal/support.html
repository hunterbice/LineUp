How can we help?

Contact LineUp for account help, privacy questions, venue corrections, or product issues.

Email support

support@get-lineup.app

Report a bug

Use the public issue tracker for reproducible product bugs that do not contain personal information.

Open issue tracker

Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account directly in LineUp under Profile > Account & Access. Support is not required to complete deletion.

Venue operators

Email support for venue corrections or authorized staff-access questions. LineUp does not grant venue access based only on a public request.
