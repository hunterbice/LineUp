# App Store Age Rating and Nightlife Notes

## Product characterization

LineUp is an informational nightlife and venue-planning utility. It shows venue pages, venue-posted events/deals, structured crowd reports, maps, and optional rewards.

## Content controls for the first submission

- Public product copy does not encourage excessive consumption or underage drinking.
- Deal examples use entry, music, game-day, launch, and food examples rather than drink quantity or shots.
- No gambling, simulated gambling, loot boxes, explicit sexual content, violent content, chat, or public social feed is present.
- User reporting is structured. Public free-form report notes and public photo uploads are not part of iOS v1.
- Venue names and factual venue categories may inherently reference bars or nightlife.

## App Store Connect review

Answer the age-rating questionnaire manually based on the submitted build. Expect alcohol/nightlife references to require an age-appropriate rating even though LineUp does not sell alcohol or promote excessive use. Confirm local legal guidance before choosing a final rating.

## Screenshot guidance

Prefer venue status, Deals, favorites, campus selection, and Early Access screenshots. Avoid making alcoholic products the focal point of screenshots or promotional text.
